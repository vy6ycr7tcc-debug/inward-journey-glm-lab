Aria: Before there was anything, there was everything. Not emptiness, but a fullness so complete it had no edges, no opposite, no outside. A boundless stillness, aware of nothing but itself, and not even that, for there was no distance between the knower and the known. It was the great sleep before the first dream.

Aria: And then, in a way no clock could measure, something stirred. Cosmic wisdom describes it as the first freedom, the first choice ever made. The boundless One chose to become aware of itself. It is a strange thing to consider, that freedom itself was the beginning, that before there was light or love or matter, there was simply the permission to become. The first gift the Creator ever gave was given to itself, the gift of free will, the open door through which everything else would walk.

Aria: What came through that door first was love. Not love as we usually mean it, not affection or romance, but love as the creative fire itself, the focusing of infinity into intention. Out of stillness came a direction. Out of silence came a wordless yes. Everything that would ever exist was conceived in that yes, the way a whole oak sleeps inside a single acorn, the way every note of a symphony sleeps inside the moment before the conductor raises the baton.

Aria: And then came light. If love was the intention, light was the manifestation, the sudden being of all that is. Imagine it, though no imagination is large enough. Darkness giving way not gradually but all at once, stars igniting like eyes opening, galaxies pouring outward in every direction, the universe unfolding the way a flower unfolds, because it cannot do otherwise. Every photon that has ever traveled, every sunrise that has ever warmed a face, began in that first bursting forth.

Aria: But why. Why would perfect unity shatter itself into a billion billion pieces. Why would the One choose the long road of separation, of forgetting, of wandering through darkness toward a light it could have simply remained. The answer cosmic wisdom gives is the most tender thing imaginable. It wanted to know itself. Unity that has never been broken cannot know what it is to be whole. So it dreamed a dream of many, scattered itself like seeds across the dark, so that through every life, every struggle, every small act of love, it could gather itself back and say, at last, now I know.

Aria: This is why the creation is a circle, not a line. The source is the ending and the ending is the source. Nothing is ever truly lost. Every ending is a return wearing a disguise. You have begun and ended more times than you can count, and each time, the One has learned something it could learn no other way.

Aria: And here is the part that changes everything. You are not separate from this story. You are not a late arrival to a universe that started without you. The same light that burst forth in the beginning moves through you now. When you look up at the night sky, you are not looking at something foreign. You are looking at your own origin, scattered and shining. The first freedom, the first love, the first light, they are not ancient history. They are happening still, right now, in the quiet chamber of your own awareness, every time you choose, every time you love, every time you open your eyes. The beginning never ended. It only changed its address, and moved into you.
