Let us begin by making the word smaller, because the word is smaller than your fear of it. A psychic greeting, in the old sense of the term, is not every dark night. It is not every run of bad luck, not every heaviness, not every hard season. Most of what we call attack is simply the ordinary friction of growing. The tradition is precise here, and that precision is good news. A greeting is an intentional sending, a focused pressure aimed at your energy, not your body. And here is the first calming fact. By far the most common greeting comes from inside the house.

The unintegrated parts of you greet the parts that are, the way a frightened dog barks at its own reflection. Old grief, old anger, the habits you thought you had outgrown press against the person you are becoming, and it can feel like being opposed from outside. Nine times out of ten, the shadow is a part of you asking to be brought back into the fold, not an enemy at the gate. Greetings from a genuinely outside source, the teaching says, are relatively rare. It takes remarkable spiritual focus to even show up on that radar.

But suppose something does come knocking. How does it work. The tradition says such a presence looks for chinks, unhealed places, unexamined appetites, the small compromises you have been avoiding. Temptations of ego or excess, left unaddressed, are the openings. Which leads to the second calming fact, worth saying slowly. The doorway only opens from the inside. Which means the lock is in your hand. A person who leaves no openings, the teaching says, will not be attacked. Not because they are hiding, but because there is nothing to grip.

And what is the protection, then. It is not a wall. It is an attitude. A firm, quiet confidence that the dark cannot ultimately touch you is itself described as an armor of light. Light does not hide you. Light is you.

Now the practice, and it is almost comically simple. When you sense a greeting, do four things. First, recognize it. Just name it, the way you would name weather. Second, send love and light, not only to the sender but to yourself. Third, give thanks. Thank the Creator for the chance to choose again, and thank the greeting itself as a compliment. You were emitting enough light to be worth distracting. Fourth, forgive. Forgive the greeter, forgive yourself, and move on with your day. Most of the time, the tradition says, that is all it takes.

If it persists, there is a deeper move. Do not push it away. Unwrap it. Embrace the energy of the seeming attack and ask what gift is folded inside the discomfort. Meet the greeting with love, the way you would hold up a lamp in a dark room to see what is really there. Love is a test the darkness cannot pass.

And here is what the greeting cannot do, however loud it gets. It cannot change who you are. It can rattle you. It cannot redefine you. Discouragement may visit; loss of self is not required.

Humanity has always had a name for this fear, and a bead for it. From ancient Greece to the villages of the Middle East, people hung blue beads and painted eyes over doorways against the evil eye, the old belief that a hostile glance could carry harm. Nobody taught these far-apart peoples to do it; the worry is that ancient. Worth remembering the next time your worry flares: you are not the first to feel watched by the dark, and the old answer was always the same. Steady the heart. Walk on. The shield was never around you. It was in you, remembered at the right moment.

Two more things, and then a benediction. You do not need a diagnosis to use the remedy. No clear line divides ordinary misfortune from a true greeting, and it does not matter. The response is the same either way. So do not go hunting your life for attacks. And keep your sense of proportion. The tradition credits one great teacher's endurance, under years of genuine pressure, partly to her replenished energies and partly, it says with enormous affection, to her sense of humor. A laugh is a kind of light.

So the benediction is this. You are forever safe where it counts. Fear will come; you are not asked to be without it, only not to be ruled by it. Hold it gently, love it as part of yourself, and walk forward anyway. If the deepest truth is love, then the choice of fearlessness is always correct. Notice it. Bless it. Release it. And keep shining. The shining was always the whole of the protection.
