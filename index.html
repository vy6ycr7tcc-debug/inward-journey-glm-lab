    How to play

    The stick, lower left
    Put your thumb anywhere low on the left. Push a little to walk, further to run.
    Look
    Drag anywhere else to look around. Tap the land and you walk there.
    The round button
    Tap to jump. Hold to take off and rise; let go to glide. In flight a tap is a wingbeat. In water it dives and rises.
    The word beside it
    What you can do here: Land, Dive, Floor, Surface.
    Hold on something
    A tree, a rock, a crystal or the ground: you go and lay your hands on it. Hold on your own heart for the genesis of light.
    Planets, stars, fruit, crystals
    Tap one to hear the voice it keeps.
    The half-moon, top right
    The narration player: play or pause, back 15 seconds, next. Its card has "Only nature" to rest every voice.
    ↺ Hear again
    In the monuments' rooms, starts the room's voice from the beginning.
    Contemplation
    Be still for about fifteen seconds and you become your own eyes, turned toward whatever moves nearby. Touch anywhere to return.
    Quiet screen
    When you don't touch the screen the controls fade away. Any touch brings them back.
    ⋮
    The map, the guide who walks you to places, autofly, sound and settings.

    About

    Some narrations in this world are interpretive adaptations of channeled material from the L/L Research archive, voiced by AI. They are artistic interpretations, not the channeling itself. This is an independent work, not affiliated with or endorsed by L/L Research. The complete archive is freely available at llresearch.org.
