Aria: Before you lie three islands, resting on a calm sea beneath a dawn sky. There is no hurry here. No destination to earn. Only the water, the light... and you. Let your breathing slow. We will visit each island in turn, and when we are done, you will know the way back by heart.

Aria: First... the Island of Mind. Drift toward it now. The forests here are made of light. Translucent trees rise around you, and pages drift through the air, turning in a wind you cannot feel. Each page carries a thought you once believed was you. Watch them pass. You do not need to read them. Mirrors float in the fog... and each one shows you a thought, so you can finally see it... and let it go. The mind is not your enemy. It is only a mirror that forgot it was reflecting the sky. Notice how the fog thins as you stop chasing. Breathe in... and lay one thought down. Breathe out... and lay another. The island grows quieter with every release. And beneath the noise, something patient has been waiting. Stillness. It was never far. Rest here a moment. Let the mind become a clear lake... and watch the sky return to it.

Rowan: The water carries you onward. Feel the rhythm of it beneath you. The next island is closer than you think.

Rowan: Now the Island of Body rises beneath you. Dark mountains, amber light along their edges, waterfalls of light pouring down in perfect silence. This is the ground of your being, the one place you have never left. The body asks for nothing but your attention. So give it. Feel the weight of yourself... the warmth in your hands... the steady drum of your heart. Every breath is the body saying: I am still here, and so are you. You have carried so much. Set it down on these stones. The mountain does not judge what you lay upon it. It only holds. Rest into it. Feel how solid you are... how real. This is not the vehicle of your journey. This is the journey. Stay a while. And let the amber light remind you: you belong exactly where you are.

Aria: One island remains. Look to the horizon. You have seen it all along.

Aria: And on the horizon... the golden gate. Concentric rings of light around a single radiant sun. This is the Island of Spirit, and it was never far. The spirit does not strive. It does not reach. It only waits, the way the sun waits behind the clouds... patient... certain... already yours. You have crossed water and forest and mountain to arrive here. But nothing here is new. Every step of the journey was the spirit, walking itself home. You are not traveling toward love. You are made of it. The gate is open. It was never closed. Step through... and come home.

Rowan: Carry this with you when you return to the noise of the world. The islands are not far. They are one breath away... one pause... one moment of remembering. The mind, the body, the spirit. All of them, yours. All of them, one. Rest now.
