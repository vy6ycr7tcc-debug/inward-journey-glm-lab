Aria: Night water. A star burns above it, too bright to be only in the sky. You are standing at the edge of a dark, still sea... and the star is looking back at you.

Aria: At the water's edge, a figure kneels, pouring two streams of light from her hands. One into the water. One onto the earth. What she pours never runs out. Watch the ripples travel... farther than your eyes can follow. Nothing true ever stops moving outward.

Aria: Around you, moths of light drift on the night air, drawn to the same brightness you are. They do not question the star. They simply turn toward it. Be like them for a while. Turn toward the light without asking it to explain itself.

Aria: Those who mapped the inner worlds called this star Hope... and then corrected themselves. Faith. Not faith in an outcome. Faith that the light is real, even here, even in dark water. The star does not argue with the night. It only shines, and the night is changed around it.

Aria: You do not need to earn the star. You only need to look up. Let its light pour through you the way water pours through open hands. One stream for the depths. One stream for the world. You are the figure at the water's edge. You always were.

Aria: When you leave this water, take the star with you. It is not above you. It was never above you. It is the light you pour.
