A throne of dark stone stands on a square of pale light. It is empty, and it is waiting for you.

This is the Emperor. The Ra contact calls him the Experience of the Mind, and says that the conscious mind is ennobled by the use of the vast resources of the unconscious.

Everything you have lived through is stored here. Not as a burden. As a treasury. Every hard winter you survived, every small courage nobody saw, every time you began again.

Sit, if only in your imagination, on the throne of your own experience. Let your life sit beside you like an honored elder.

Ask it: what have you made of me? And listen, the way you would listen to someone you respect.
