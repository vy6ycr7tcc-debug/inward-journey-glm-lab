Before you lies the water, dark and wide, and far off, almost imagined, an island.

You have stood on this shore before. Every life has a moment like this. The noise behind you, the unknown ahead, and the quiet question underneath it all.

The old transcripts describe the beginning of the mind as the untaught seeker. The beginner's mind. Reaching, reaching, and again reaching.

You do not need to know what the island holds. You do not need to be ready. You only need to begin.

Take one slow breath. Feel the ground under your feet, and then let it go.

The water is waiting.
