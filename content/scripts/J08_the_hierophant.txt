An archway of three stones, and beneath it a teacher with no face. Only a listening stillness.

This is the Hierophant. In the Ra contact he is the Significator of the Mind, the meaning your mind has made of everything it has touched.

Ra says the heart of the mind is a dynamic entity which absorbs, and seeks, and attempts to learn. That is you. Not the thoughts that pass through, but the one who watches them gather.

Stand under the arch and notice the shape of your own inner teacher. It does not lecture. It does not scold. It simply keeps asking, with infinite patience: what did you learn?

Let the question hang in the air like incense. You do not have to answer it today.
