A vessel of pure light waits at the edge of a long, straight road that runs to the horizon and beyond it.

This is the Chariot. Ra calls it the Great Way of the Mind, a reflection and a summary of all six stations you have walked.

Look back for a moment. The reaching of the Magician. The stillness of the Priestess. The wild garden of the Empress. The throne of the Emperor. The archway of the Hierophant. The crossroads of the Lovers. They were never separate places. They were always one road, and you were always walking it.

Step into the vessel when you are ready. The way forward is the way inward, and it has been waiting for you all along.
