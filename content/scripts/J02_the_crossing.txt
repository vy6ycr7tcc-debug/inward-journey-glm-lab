You are in the water now, and it is holding you.

Do not fight it. The deep mind is not crossed by force. It is crossed the way you cross any water in the dark, one gentle movement at a time, trusting what you cannot see.

As you swim, things may float up toward you. A memory. A feeling you thought you had finished with. Do not chase them, and do not push them away. Let them drift past like leaves on a current.

The transcripts say that beneath the surface of every mind there is a sea, and that everything you have ever been is still down there, shining quietly.

Each stroke is a small act of trust. The island is closer than it looks. Keep going.
