The island rises out of the water to meet you.

Step onto the sand. Stand still for a moment. Notice how the noise of the outer world grows thinner here, the way mist thins in morning light.

Inland, seven shapes are waiting. Seven doors, each one a mirror for a different part of your own mind. You do not need to understand them yet. You do not need to open them in any order. Walk slowly. The island will show you whatever you are ready to see.

There is no test here. There is nothing to win. There is only the quiet work of looking inward, and the strange, gentle joy of being looked back at.

Begin whenever you are ready.
