Turn back toward the water. The island grows smaller behind you, but nothing you found here stays behind.

You came across this water seeking something. You are crossing back as someone. That is the whole of it, and it is enough.

The star you followed here goes with you now. It was never on the island. It was never in the sky. It was the light you were carrying the entire time, and the journey only taught you how to see it.

Swim gently. The shore you left is still there, and the noise is still there, but you will not stand on it the same way again.

Go in peace, and remember the way back. The water will always be here.
