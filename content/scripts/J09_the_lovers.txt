Two paths cross here beneath a bright star, and where they meet, a garden of two trees grows from a single root.

These are the Lovers. The Ra contact calls this the Transformation of the Mind. The old transcripts speak of two equal choices, neither greater than the other, and the sacred weight of choosing.

Every transformation begins this way. Not with an answer, but with a crossroads. To become something new, something old must be released, and the choice is always yours.

You do not have to choose now. Only stand at the crossing. Feel the two paths pulling gently, like two hands.

And ask yourself: if I were free, truly free, which way would my heart lean?
