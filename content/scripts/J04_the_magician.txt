Before you stands the first figure, one arm raised toward the sky, one hand pointing down toward the earth. Between the two, a current of light.

This is the Magician. In the old Ra contact he is called the Matrix of the Mind, and he is described as the untaught seeker. The beginner's mind. Reaching, reaching, and again reaching.

All magic begins as desire. Before anything is built, before anything is understood, something in you reaches.

Stand here a while, and ask yourself the oldest question in the world. What am I truly reaching for?

Do not answer quickly. Hold the question lightly, the way you would hold a bird. It will tell you when it trusts you.
