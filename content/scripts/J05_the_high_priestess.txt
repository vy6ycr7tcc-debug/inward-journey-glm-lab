Between two pillars sits a still figure, half hidden behind a veil, with a book of quiet wisdom resting in her lap.

This is the High Priestess. The Ra contact calls her the Potentiator of the Mind, that which receives your desire and enables it, by reflection and by blessing.

She does not strive. She does not hurry. She waits, the way deep water waits, and everything you bring to her comes back clearer.

The deepest answers do not arrive when you chase them. They rise on their own, in silence, the way stars appear when you stop looking directly at them.

Sit with her a while. Rest your question in the quiet. And notice, without forcing it, what begins to surface.
