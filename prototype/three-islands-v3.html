Three Islands

Mind, Body, and Spirit rest in the ether as three islands, each holding seven places. Go to what calls you, and be still there.

Sound and voice begin softly. Both can be turned off.

Drag to look around. Tap a glowing place, or tap the ground, to glide there. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or W A S D.

Near a place, be still. It opens and speaks. A faint column of light marks the next place in Ra's teaching order, if you want to follow it.

Places lets you travel anywhere and hear a line again. Nothing is scored or saved.
