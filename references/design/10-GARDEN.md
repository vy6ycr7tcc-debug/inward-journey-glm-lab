# 10 — Garden

## The Feeling

A contemplative garden. Lush, green, alive — but still. Not a wild jungle, not a manicured park. A garden that feels tended by no one, growing in its own sacred order.

## Its Role

The garden is the **reference standard** for the series' natural scenes. The tree station (file 05) was color-matched to the garden. If you're calibrating what "living green-gold" looks like in this world, look at the garden.

## The Palette

Greens with golden light filtering through. Dappled shade. The light should feel like it's coming through leaves — warm, patterned, alive.

This is the palette anchor: when the tree station's canopy glows golden-green, it's glowing *like the garden*.

## Narration

No dedicated garden narration track exists yet. The garden serves as a visual palette reference and contemplative space.

## What to Avoid

- Overly saturated green. This isn't a cartoon forest.
- Visible gardening — no fences, no paths with edges, no flowerbeds. It should feel natural, not landscaped.
- Clutter. Even in lushness, there's restraint. Every plant earns its place.
