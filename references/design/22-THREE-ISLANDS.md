# 22 — The Three Islands (Mind, Body, Spirit)

## The Structure

The 22 archetypes are organized as **three islands** — Mind, Body, and Spirit — with **seven archetypes each**, plus The Choice as the 22nd.

This is the deeper architecture beneath the temple galleries (file 02). The galleries show all 21 archetypes, but they're grouped by island:

- **Island of Mind** (7 archetypes) — thought, reflection, mirrors, pages, libraries
- **Island of Body** (7 archetypes) — mountains, physicality, the tree station lives here
- **Island of Spirit** (7 archetypes) — light, vastness, galaxies, transcendence

**Plus The Choice** (#22) — which belongs to none of the islands and completes the set.

## Each Island Has Its Own Character

### Island of Mind
Drifting pages. Mirrors (which should be visually distinct and actually reflective — the prototype's mirrors read as floating pages, which was noted as insufficient). Libraries. Dream landscapes. Fog. The feeling of thought — intangible, reflective, layered.

### Island of Body
Mountains. The tree station (file 05). Physicality, groundedness, the tangible world. Faster waterfalls (more kinetic energy than the other islands). The feeling of *being* in a body — weight, breath, growth.

### Island of Spirit
Breathing rings. Light. Vastness. Galaxies (file 11). The feeling of expansion beyond the self — not escape, but inclusion of everything.

## Island Transitions

Moving between islands is a **cinematic dive** — the camera accelerates, the horizon tilts, rings expand, particles bloom. It's a transition that *feels* like traveling between worlds, not like loading a new level.

## The Content Gap

The archive analysis revealed that **Body is the thinnest category** — far fewer source transcripts deal with body themes compared to mind, spirit, and emotion. This means Island of Body may need more original creative development to feel as rich as the other two.
