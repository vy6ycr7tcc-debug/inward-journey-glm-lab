# 07 — Igloo

## The Feeling

A shelter of ice and snow. Contemplative, quiet, luminous. The ice should glow from within — blue-white light filtering through. You're inside something that protects you from a vast cold outside, and the contrast makes the interior feel womb-like, safe.

## The User's Verdict on the Prototype

"Awful tbh."

The first attempt looked like a low-poly game asset — faceted, cheap, uninviting. It didn't feel like ice. It didn't feel like shelter. It felt like a 3D model of an igloo.

## What Went Wrong (and the Nuance)

The problem wasn't the shape. It was the *materiality*. Real ice in a contemplative context should feel:

- **Translucent** — light passes through it, glows inside it
- **Smooth** — wind-worn, not faceted. The prototype's hard polygon edges killed it.
- **Luminous** — the walls should emit a soft blue-white glow, like the ice itself is a light source

The prototype looked like someone modeled an igloo in a beginner 3D tutorial. The fix isn't more polygons — it's making the ice feel like *ice*: translucent, glowing, smooth, cold but inviting.

## What to Aim For

- You're inside. The walls curve around you. Light filters through the ice — soft, blue-white, diffused.
- Outside (visible through an opening or translucent walls): vast white expanse, maybe aurora, definitely cold.
- The contrast between the warm safe interior and the vast cold exterior is the emotional content.
- Silence. Or near-silence. Wind, very faint.

## Narration

No dedicated igloo narration track exists yet. The scene is currently atmosphere-driven. If narration is added, it should be sparse — the ice and silence carry the meaning.

## What to Avoid

- Faceted low-poly look. If you can see the polygons, it's wrong.
- Making it feel like a game level. No "ice cave adventure" energy.
- Warm lighting inside. This is the one scene where cool light is correct — but it should be *luminous* cool, not dead blue.
