# 23 — The Wanderer & Home (Sunrise Lake)

## What This Is

Before the temple, before the islands — there is **home**. A sunrise lake. The user controls a **luminous wanderer** — a being of light — walking across the water as the sun rises.

This is the starting point. The hub. The place the user returns to.

## The Wanderer

- A **glowing figure** — not a detailed human, but a luminous presence
- The user **controls** it — walks it, explores with it
- It moves with the animation language (file 17): eased, gliding, never robotic
- It's the user's presence in the world — "you" as light

## The Sunrise Lake

- Water stretching to the horizon
- Sun rising — warm gold light, long reflections
- Gentle piano (minimal, contemplative)
- Minimal UI — almost nothing on screen. The world is the interface.

## The Interaction

The user can **click beautiful illustrated objects** — floating framed versions of the ink drawings (art/). When clicked, they open — revealing... more. A deeper layer. A story. A meditation.

The user is open to drawing more assets themselves. The illustrated objects can grow over time.

## Why It Matters

The temple tour (files 01-03) is **guided** — the path leads you. But the broader game is **exploratory** — the user wanders, discovers, clicks, contemplates at their own pace.

The wanderer is the difference between watching a film and being in a world. The user explicitly wants the latter: "a complete, end-to-end interactive meditation game rather than another rendered animation."

## The Luminous Orb (Diegetic Narrator)

In the temple tour, the narration isn't just a voice — it has a **visual form**: a luminous orb that acts as the guide. The orb moves through the space, and the voice comes from it. This makes the narration *present* in the world, not overlaid on top of it.
