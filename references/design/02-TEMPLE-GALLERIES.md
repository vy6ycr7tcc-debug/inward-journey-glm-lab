# 02 — Archetype Galleries

## The Feeling

On both sides of the hall, set into the walls, are shrines. Twenty-one of them. One for each archetype (seven each for Mind, Body, and Spirit — see file 22).

Each one stops you. Not because something jumps out, but because something *glows* — a presence made of light, hovering in a niche, and you understand without being told: this one matters. Contemplate it.

## The Layout

Distributed on the left and right (roughly 10-11 per side). Each set into the temple wall as a niche or alcove — not freestanding, not in the middle of the path. They're part of the architecture.

The S-weave (file 01) reveals them in sequence. You sway left — a shrine appears between the columns. You sway right — another one. The temple shows them to you one at a time. You don't choose the order; the path does.

## Each Shrine

A shrine is three things:

1. **The hologram** (file 04) — the archetype as projected light. This IS the shrine. Everything else is staging.
2. **Minimal staging** — a plinth, a pool of light on the floor, a subtle glow source. Pick one. Not all three.
3. **Space** — visual and temporal. The camera lingers. The shrine has room to breathe.

## The 22 Archetypes

The Fool, The Magician, The High Priestess, The Empress, The Emperor, The Hierophant, The Lovers, The Chariot, Strength, The Hermit, Wheel of Fortune, Justice, The Hanged Man, Death, Temperance, The Devil, The Tower, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, Judgement, The World.

## Nuance: They Shouldn't Be Identical

Each archetype has its own energy. The shrines should reflect this — not with different architecture, but with subtle shifts. Warmer or cooler light. Brighter or more subdued glow. A tall narrow niche vs. a wide one.

The user should feel, even peripherally, that each station is *someone* — a distinct presence — not a copy-pasted template.

## What to Avoid

- Identical stations. This kills the sacred feeling instantly.
- Clutter around the hologram. The light IS the content.
- Putting any archetype content in the final room. The galleries are the only place archetypes live.
- Making them feel like museum displays. They're shrines, not exhibits.

## Narration

The TEMPLE track ("One Road, Twenty-Two Stations") covers the galleries — the 26 scene cues include the archetype stations. Each station's narration should align with its hologram activation. When the voice speaks of an archetype, that archetype's hologram ignites.
