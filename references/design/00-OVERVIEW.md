# 00 — Overview

## What This Is

**Inward Journey** — a contemplative 3D art series about the archetypes of Mind, Body, and Spirit. Not a game. Not a lecture. A door.

The feeling we're after: you walk into a sacred space and something in your chest loosens. Ethereal calm. Light you can almost touch. Geometry that feels meaningful without telling you what to believe.

**Universal, nonreligious, non-dogmatic.** This is critical. The work points inward without imprinting any specific belief system.

## The Visual North Star

The user's own ink drawings (2014–2016, in `art/`). Fine-line sacred geometry. Intricate, precise, spiritual without being religious. Spanish and English text woven into the linework. Themes of liberation, love, faith.

You don't copy these. You absorb what they *feel* like — the patience in every line, the way geometry becomes meditation — and you build 3D spaces that feel like that.

## The Three Pillars

Every creative decision should serve one of these:

1. **Ethereal calm** — the nervous system should downshift. Nothing jarring, nothing loud, nothing rushed.
2. **Light as presence** — light isn't illumination, it's a character. Gold, warmth, glow. Things made of light feel alive; things made of matter feel like sets.
3. **Restraint** — the most powerful move is often removing something. The user has repeatedly asked to take things *out*, not put things in.

## What We're Building (in order)

1. **Temple hall** — the walk in, with its signature weaving path
2. **Archetype galleries** — 22 stations, both sides, each a small shrine
3. **Tarot holograms** — the cards as projected light (not photos)
4. **Final room** — the Choice, alone, in a shaft of light
5. **Tree station** — a 7-part story about breaking and rebirth
6. **Duat** — the underworld, de-boxed and organic
7. **Igloo, desert, shore, garden, galaxies** — the remaining contemplative spaces

## The Voices

Two narrators alternate — one per section. Female (warm, American) and male (satiny, soothing). Slow. Contemplative. They guide, they don't lecture.

## The Platform That Matters

The user experiences this on **iPhone**. If it doesn't work beautifully on iPhone Safari, it doesn't work. This isn't a secondary target — it's the primary one.
