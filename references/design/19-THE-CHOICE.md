# 19 — The Choice (Finale Detail)

## What the Choice Actually Is

The Choice is not a single button or a binary decision. It's a **reflective ending with three options**:

1. **"I choose to love"**
2. **"I choose to rest"**
3. **"I choose not to choose — yet"**

Each option changes the atmosphere and then releases the user. This isn't a quiz with a right answer — it's a contemplative moment where the user names their intention.

## What Each Choice Does

### "I choose to love"
The atmosphere shifts toward warmth, expansion, radiance. The light grows. Then the user is released to wander.

### "I choose to rest"
The user is taken to the **Tree of Life** — a hilltop tree with a motherly wisp (rose-gold, nurturing presence). A place to sit and rest. This is a complete scene, not just a fade-out.

**The Tree of Life rest scene:** A glowing tree on a coastal hill. Golden-green canopy. Falling leaves. Fireflies. Warm light in the trunk. A mossy rest point facing water and mountain silhouettes. A small rose-gold wisp — motherly, comforting. The user sits. Rests. This is the reward for choosing rest.

### "I choose not to choose — yet"
The atmosphere softens, holds space for uncertainty. The user is released without pressure. Not choosing is also a valid choice.

## The Procedural Imagery

When the Choice activates, the scene comes alive with **creation imagery**:
- Birds circling
- Distant life on the horizon
- Crystals rising
- Rings expanding
- Vertical beams of light

This is the "Vision of Creation" (file 16) manifesting — luminous forms gathering, dissolving, reforming. The Choice doesn't just change a variable; it changes the *world*.

## Nuance

The Choice is the culmination of the entire temple journey. The user has walked the hall, contemplated 21 archetypes, arrived at the final room. The three options should feel like natural completions of that journey — not like a menu, but like the temple asking: "What will you carry forward?"

The "not yet" option is important. It honors the user who isn't ready. This isn't a test.
