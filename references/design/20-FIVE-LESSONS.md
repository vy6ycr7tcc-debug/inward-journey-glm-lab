# 20 — The Five Lessons

## What These Are

Beyond the temple tour and the Choice, there are **five narrated lesson scenes**. Each is a contemplative experience built around a specific theme, with full narration.

These are grounded in the L/L Research channeling archive — the same philosophical source as the archetypes.

## The Five Lessons

### LESSON-03: "The Untying" (Forgiveness)
About forgiveness — not as pardoning, but as untying the knot that binds you to the hurt. Letting go of the grip.

### LESSON-04: "Without Price" (Unconditional Love)
About love and service without expectation. Giving without keeping score. The practice of offering without needing return.

### LESSON-05: "The Fire in the Hand" (Anger)
About anger and the choice of what to do with it. The fire is real — the question is whether you wield it or it wields you.

### LESSON-06: "What Is" (Acceptance)
About acceptance — seeing what is without needing it to be different. This is not approval. It's clarity.

### LESSON-07: "The Dark and the Lantern" (Faith)
About faith and trust without full visibility. Walking forward with only a lantern's worth of light. Trusting the path when you can't see the destination.

## Each Lesson Is a Scene

These aren't just audio tracks. Each lesson has its own **visual scene** — a contemplative space designed around its theme. The visuals and the narration work together (see file 16, Vision of Creation).
