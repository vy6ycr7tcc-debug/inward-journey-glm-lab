# 11 — Galaxies

## The Feeling

The cosmic scale. Stars, nebulae, the vastness. Contemplation of the infinite — the "Spirit" aspect of the Mind/Body/Spirit trilogy.

## Open Decision

**Whether this scene gets narration or is music-only is still undecided.**

The argument for narration: the series uses voice to guide contemplation, and the cosmic scale might need that guidance to feel personal rather than just pretty.

The argument for silence/music-only: at the cosmic scale, words might diminish the awe. Sometimes the most profound response to the infinite is to shut up and look.

**This is a creative decision for the build.** Try it both ways if possible. The user's taste leans toward restraint — when in doubt, less narration.

## Visual Nuance

- This should feel like *being among* the galaxies, not looking at a picture of space.
- Slow drift. The stars move almost imperceptibly. The scale should make the user feel both tiny and connected.
- Avoid the "screensaver nebula" look — over-saturated purples and blues that scream stock space art. The palette should feel like it belongs in the same universe as the golden temple and the green garden.
- Light, not darkness. Even in space, the feeling should be luminous, not void-like.
