# 08 — Desert

## The Feeling

Vast, golden, still. A desert at the contemplative hour — not harsh noon, but the warm light of morning or late afternoon. Dunes rolling to the horizon. The air itself feels golden.

## What Exists

The prototype restyled the desert — the base is there. Dunes, warm palette, atmospheric.

## What's Missing

**Floating vegetation.** The desert needs life — not a forest, but the suggestion of it. Plants or organic forms that drift or hover, catching the light. This is the surreal/contemplative element that lifts the desert from "landscape" to "vision."

The nuance: these shouldn't look like tumbleweeds or realistic plants. They should feel like *presences* — organic, luminous, slightly impossible. Think: seed pods made of light, drifting. Or ghostly plants, half-transparent, moving very slowly in a wind you can't feel.

## The Palette

Gold, amber, warm sand. The sky should be luminous — not blue, but warm white-gold at the horizon deepening upward. The prototype got the warmth right; preserve it.

## Narration

No dedicated desert narration track exists yet. The scene is currently atmosphere-driven.

## What to Avoid

- Harshness. This isn't a survival desert. No blinding sun, no cracked earth close-ups.
- Emptiness without purpose. The vastness should feel contemplative, not boring. The floating vegetation and the light are what keep it alive.
- Realism. This is a vision of a desert, not a photograph of one.
